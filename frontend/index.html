Crew Advisory Tool — Not an Official Dispatch System
PULSE provides hospital routing suggestions based on crew-reported wall time data. This tool is intended to support — not replace — crew judgment, medical direction, or official dispatch protocols. Data reflects crew submissions from the last 30 min and may not represent current conditions.
About

EMS Wall Time is an independent decision-support aid for EMS crews. It is not affiliated with any hospital system, county agency, or NY State Department of Health, and does not constitute medical advice or official operational direction.

Data

All estimates are crew-reported within a rolling 30-min window. Data is unverified and may not reflect diversion status, trauma activations, or sudden ED surges. Actual conditions depend on the receiving facility at time of arrival.

Responsibility

All routing and destination decisions remain the sole responsibility of the attending crew and medical director. In any conflict with official dispatch or medical direction — defer to official channels.

Liability

Use is voluntary. The EMS Wall Time team accepts no liability for patient outcomes or operational decisions made using this application.
